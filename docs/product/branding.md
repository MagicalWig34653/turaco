# Turaco Brand Direction

**Status:** Working direction

Turaco takes its visual identity from turacos: distinctive birds with strong green plumage and vivid red/blue/violet accents. The product UI should be recognizable without becoming visually noisy.

## Brand principles

- Professional internal-IT workspace first; mascot aesthetics must never make operational screens feel childish.
- Neutral graphite/slate surfaces carry most of the interface.
- Turaco-inspired colors provide navigation, module identity, diagrams, illustrations and selected emphasis.
- Semantic state colors remain consistent and are never repurposed merely for branding.
- Accessibility contrast is mandatory in light and dark themes.

## Initial palette tokens

These are design tokens, not immutable brand assets:

- `turaco-ink`: `#172033`
- `turaco-leaf`: `#169B72`
- `turaco-wing`: `#635BCE`
- `turaco-sky`: `#168DA3`
- `turaco-coral`: `#E45B5F`
- `turaco-gold`: `#D99A32`

Do not encode domain meaning directly into these brand names. Module accents may use them, while status colors use separate semantic tokens.

## Naming

- Turaco
