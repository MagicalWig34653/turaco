# Turaco Name Clearance

**Status:** Preliminary only — not legal clearance

`Turaco` is the selected repository/project name. Before a public launch, trademark filing, paid marketing or domain investment, perform a formal clearance covering at minimum EUIPO, DPMA and relevant WIPO designations in the intended software/SaaS/service classes.

## Preliminary web findings (2026-09-30)

The word is **not globally unused**:

- `Turaco` is the name of an established insurance technology company with its own software platform.
- `Turaco Cloud` is used by an application-modernization/cloud software offering.
- `Turaco Soft` is used by a software-development company.
- Other trademark registrations exist in unrelated product classes.

These findings do not decide legal registrability or infringement, but they create SEO/name-confusion risk and make formal clearance mandatory.

## Repository rule

Until clearance is complete:

- use `Turaco` as the project/product name in code and documentation;
- do not claim that the mark is registered or exclusively owned;
- do not add trademark symbols;
- do not purchase expensive brand assets solely on the assumption that the name is clear;
- keep renaming technically straightforward by avoiding the brand in domain terminology and database schema names where it adds no value.
